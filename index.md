# Momentum Tracking — Practice Management for Solo Australian PTs

**Stop guessing, start knowing.**

Momentum Tracking is the only practice management app built specifically for solo personal trainers in Australia. Handle your clients, sessions, packages, revenue, invoices, expenses, and BAS reporting — all in one place.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Most PTs are running their business on a patchwork of spreadsheets, notes apps, and generic invoicing tools. Nothing talks to each other. Revenue is unclear. Tax time is a nightmare. Client history lives in your head.

Momentum Tracking replaces all of that with a single, purpose-built system.

---

## Features

### Client Management
Store client profiles, health history, contact details, and session notes in one place.

### Session Tracking
Log every session against a client. View full session history at a glance.

### Package and Prepaid Credit Management
Sell session packs and let the app handle credit deductions automatically. No more manually counting remaining sessions.

### Revenue Tracking
See exactly what you've earned — by client, by period, by package type. Know your numbers without opening a spreadsheet.

### Invoicing
Create and send professional invoices to clients directly from the app.

### Expense Tracking
Log business expenses with categories and GST fields. Everything you need come tax time.

### BAS and GST Reporting
Built for Australian sole traders. Generate quarterly BAS-ready GST summaries aligned with ATO requirements — in minutes, not hours.

### Leads Pipeline
Track prospective clients from first enquiry to signed-up. Never lose a lead in your inbox again.

### Stripe Payments
Accept card payments from clients directly through the app.

## Who It's For

Momentum Tracking is built for **solo personal trainers in Australia** who:

- Operate as sole traders
- Have 5–40 active clients
- Train clients in-person, online, or both
- Need GST/BAS reporting that actually works for their tax obligations
- Want one tool, not five

It is **not** built for PT studios, gyms, or fitness franchises.
